Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

What the Tool collects

The Milanka Tarot publishing tool connects to one TikTok account: the Milanka Tarot account. When that account authorizes the Tool, TikTok provides the account's open ID, an access token, a refresh token and the granted permissions (user.info.basic, video.upload).

The Tool does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other TikTok users, and it does not use cookies or analytics on this site.

How it is used

The tokens are used only to upload daily post drafts (images, a video and the caption) to the Milanka Tarot TikTok inbox and to check their upload status.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted in the Tool's database and are never shared, sold or used for advertising. Generated images and videos are stored in the project's own cloud storage.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the account stays connected. Revoking the Tool's access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions) stops all access; the stored tokens are then deleted on request.
